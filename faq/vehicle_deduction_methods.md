# Vehicle Deduction Methods: Cents per km vs Logbook

When claiming motor vehicle expenses on your tax return, the ATO allows sole traders and individuals to choose between two methods. FareKeep calculates both methods for each vehicle and lets you pick the one that gives you the best outcome.

## Cents per Kilometre Method

This is the simpler option.

- You claim a **fixed rate per business kilometre** driven during the financial year.
- The current ATO rate is displayed in FareKeep and is updated automatically each year.
- The maximum you can claim is **5,000 business kilometres** per vehicle per year (ATO cap).
- You do **not** need to keep receipts for individual vehicle running costs (fuel, tyres, etc.) to use this method — but you still need to be able to show how you calculated your business kilometres.

**Best for:** Vehicles with low running costs, low business use, or when your total business driving is under 5,000 km for the year.

## Logbook Method

This method uses your actual vehicle running costs and your logbook's business-use percentage.

- FareKeep adds up all the vehicle-related expenses you've [linked to this vehicle](vehicle_linked_expenses) during the financial year (fuel, insurance, registration, servicing, tyres, etc.).
- It then multiplies that total by your logbook's **business-use percentage** to calculate your deduction.
- There is **no kilometre cap** — you can claim based on all your actual costs.
- You must maintain a valid logbook (either a 12-week ATO logbook or an open-ended logbook in FareKeep) to use this method.

**Best for:** Vehicles with high running costs, high business use, or when your business driving exceeds 5,000 km for the year.

## How FareKeep Helps You Choose

In the [Tax Wizard](tax_wizard), FareKeep displays both deduction amounts side-by-side for each vehicle:

| Method | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Cents per km | e.g. $3,425 |
| Logbook | e.g. $4,870 |

FareKeep **automatically selects the method that gives you the higher deduction** by default. You can override this selection at any time by tapping the other option.

Your selected method is saved and used when generating your Tax Summary PDF.

## Important Notes

- You can use **different methods for different vehicles**. For example, you might use Cents per km for your second car and Logbook for your primary work vehicle.
- Switching methods does not change your underlying data — FareKeep always calculates both, and you can switch back at any time before generating your report.
- The method selection is per **financial year** — you can choose differently each year.

*For official guidance on motor vehicle deduction methods, visit the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/deductions-you-can-claim/vehicles-trips-and-travel).*
