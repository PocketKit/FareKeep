# How to Add Income & Expenses

Every dollar you earn and spend in your business should be recorded in FareKeep. This helps you stay on top of your finances and makes tax time significantly easier.

## Adding a Transaction

1. Tap the **+** (plus) button on the Dashboard or the Transactions screen.
2. At the top of the form, toggle between **Income** and **Expense** using the switch.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Date** — defaults to today, but you can change it.
   - **Amount** — the total amount including GST (if applicable).
   - **Description** — a short note to remind you what this was for (e.g., "Uber fare earnings" or "Fuel at BP").

### For Expenses

You will also see:
- **Category** — select the type of expense (e.g., Fuel, Tolls, Phone, Insurance). You can add new categories from **Settings → Master Data**.
- **Claimable %** — how much of this expense is for business use. If it's 100% business, leave the toggle on "Fully Claimable." If it's partially personal, switch to manual entry and set the percentage, or choose "Automatic" to have FareKeep calculate it from your logbook data.

### For Income

You will also see:
- **Payment Method** — how you were paid (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cash, PayPal). You can add new methods from **Settings → Master Data**.

## GST

If your business is GST-registered, toggle on **GST Included** to have FareKeep automatically calculate the GST component (1/11th of the total amount). You can also manually override the GST amount if needed.

## Saving as a Draft

Don't have all the details right now? Tap **Save as Draft** instead. You can come back later to fill in the missing information. See [Saving Draft Transactions & Trips](draft_records) for more details.

*Tip: Snap a photo of your receipt before you lose it! You can attach it directly to the transaction — see [Attaching receipts & documents](receipts_and_attachments).*
