# BAS Due Date Reminders

Missing a BAS lodgement deadline can result in penalties from the ATO. FareKeep can send you push notifications before each due date so you never forget.

## How to Enable Reminders

1. Go to **Settings → BAS Due Dates & Reminders**.
2. Toggle on **Enable BAS Reminders**.
3. Your device will ask for permission to send notifications — tap **Allow**.
4. Select your **Lodgement Frequency**:
   - **Quarterly** — for most businesses with GST turnover under $20 million.
   - **Monthly** — mandatory for businesses with GST turnover of $20 million or more (optional for smaller businesses).
   - **Yearly** — for businesses voluntarily registered for GST with turnover under $75,000.

## How It Works

Once enabled, FareKeep schedules local notifications on your device to remind you before each upcoming BAS due date. These notifications are processed entirely on your device — no data is sent to any server.

The screen also displays a list of **upcoming due dates** for the current financial year so you can plan ahead.

## Disabling Reminders

Simply toggle off **Enable BAS Reminders** on the same screen. All scheduled notifications will be cancelled immediately.

*For the official ATO lodgement schedule, visit the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
