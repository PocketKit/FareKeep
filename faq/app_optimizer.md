# What Does the App Optimizer Do?

Over time, your app's storage can grow as you add more receipt photos and documents. The App Optimizer helps you reclaim space and keep things running smoothly.

You can find it in **Settings → App Optimizer**.

## Image Conversion

When FareKeep was first released, receipt photos were stored in standard formats like JPEG and PNG. The App Optimizer can batch-convert these older images to **WebP format**, which is significantly smaller without any noticeable loss in quality.

**How to use it:**
1. Open **Settings → App Optimizer**.
2. Tap **Convert Images to WebP**.
3. The app will scan all your transaction attachments and convert any JPEG or PNG images to WebP.
4. A progress bar will show you how many images have been converted.

Images that are already in WebP format are automatically skipped.

## Large PDF Audit

Some PDF receipts (such as scanned documents or detailed invoices) can be unexpectedly large. The PDF Audit tool scans your attachments and identifies any PDF files that exceed a reasonable size threshold.

**How to use it:**
1. Open **Settings → App Optimizer**.
2. Tap **Scan for Large PDFs**.
3. The app will list any oversized PDF attachments along with the transaction they belong to.
4. You can then choose to compress them individually.

## When Should I Use This?

- **Before backing up** — smaller attachments mean faster and more reliable cloud backups.
- **When your device is low on storage** — reclaiming space from old images can make a meaningful difference.
- **After importing data from an older version** — if you upgraded from an earlier version of FareKeep, your older attachments may benefit from conversion.
