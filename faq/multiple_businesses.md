# Managing Multiple Businesses

If you operate more than one business — for example, you drive for Uber and also run a freelance delivery service — FareKeep lets you maintain completely separate records for each one.

## How It Works

Each business profile has its own:
- **Transactions** (income and expenses)
- **Logbooks and trips**
- **Vehicles**
- **Master Data** (expense categories, payment methods, trip purposes)
- **Reports** (GST and Tax Summary)

This means the data from one business never mixes with another.

## Creating a New Business Profile

1. On the **Dashboard**, tap the business name at the top of the screen.
2. In the dropdown that appears, tap **Add Business**.
3. Fill in the new business details (name, ABN, business type, GST status).
4. Tap **Save**.

## Switching Between Businesses

1. Tap the business name at the top of the **Dashboard**.
2. Select the business you want to view from the dropdown list.

The entire app — including transactions, logbooks, and reports — will switch to show data for the selected business.

## Editing or Deleting a Business

- To edit a business profile, go to **Settings → Profile Details**.
- To delete a business, go to **Settings → Advanced Settings → Delete Business Profile**. This will permanently remove the profile and all its associated data.
