# What are Trip Purposes?

Categorising your trips helps you organise your logbook and justify the business nature of the travel to the ATO.

Common trip purposes include:
- **Employee - Work:** Traveling between different workplaces or client sites.
- **Employee - Self-Education:** Travel related to work-related study.
- **Private Use:** Personal trips (e.g., grocery shopping, commuting from home to your regular workplace).

You can add, edit, or delete trip purposes in **Settings → Master Data → Trip Purposes**.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
