# What is a Business Activity Statement (BAS)?

A Business Activity Statement (BAS) is a form submitted to the ATO by registered businesses to report and pay their tax obligations, including GST, PAYG instalments, and PAYG withholding.

If you are registered for GST, you must lodge a BAS. Depending on your business size, you may need to lodge your BAS monthly, quarterly, or annually.

FareKeep's **GST Report** generates the exact figures you need to fill out your BAS (e.g., G1 Total Sales, 1A GST on Sales, 1B GST on Purchases).

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
