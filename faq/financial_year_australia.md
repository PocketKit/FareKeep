# Australian Financial Year Explained

In Australia, the standard financial year (also known as the tax year or fiscal year) runs from **1 July to 30 June** of the following year.

For example, the 2024–25 financial year starts on 1 July 2024 and ends on 30 June 2025.

FareKeep automatically organises your dashboard, reports, and archives based on this July–June cycle.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
