# Understanding Your Reports

FareKeep can generate professional reports that summarise your financial data — ready to hand to your accountant or use when lodging your tax return. There are two main report types.

## GST Report (BAS)

This report is designed for GST-registered businesses and helps you prepare your Business Activity Statement (BAS).

**What it shows:**
- Total GST collected on income
- Total GST paid on expenses
- Net GST payable (or refundable)
- A breakdown by quarter (Q1–Q4), month, or the full financial year

**How to generate it:**
1. Go to **Settings → GST Report (BAS)**.
2. Select the **Financial Year** and **reporting period** (Quarterly, Monthly, or Yearly).
3. Tap the **PDF** button to generate a downloadable PDF report, or the **Excel** button for a spreadsheet version.

The PDF will open directly on your device so you can review, save, or share it.

## Tax Summary Report

This report gives you a complete picture of your income, expenses, and deductions for a given financial year — ideal for your annual tax return.

**What it shows:**
- **ATO ITR Label Mapping** — your figures are mapped to standard ATO Individual Tax Return labels (e.g., Item 1: Salary, Item 15: Business Income, D1: Motor Vehicle Expenses, D5: Other Expenses), so your accountant can quickly transfer the numbers.
- Total income by payment method
- Total expenses by category
- Motor vehicle deductions per vehicle, showing the selected method (Cents per km or Logbook) and the deduction amount for each
- General (non-vehicle) business expense deductions
- Salary, interest, depreciation, and PAYG instalment amounts (if entered via the Tax Wizard)
- **Estimated Tax Outcome** — including estimated income tax, Medicare levy, HELP/HECS repayment (if applicable), and a projected refund or amount payable
- Itemised lists of all business income and expenses
- A business trip log showing all logbook trip data for the period

**How to generate it:**
1. Go to **Settings → Tax Summary Report**.
2. Select the **Financial Year**.
3. Tap **Open Tax Form** to complete the [Tax Wizard](faq/tax_wizard) — this collects your salary, interest, and other details.
4. Once the Tax Wizard is complete, return to the Tax Report screen and tap **Generate PDF**.

## Tips

- **Complete the Tax Wizard first.** The Tax Summary Report is most useful when you've entered all your additional income and deduction details through the Tax Wizard. Without it, the estimated tax calculation will only include your business data.
- **Check for drafts first.** If you have any draft transactions or trips, they will be excluded from your reports. FareKeep will show a warning banner if drafts exist in the reporting period.
- **Compare vehicle methods.** In the Tax Wizard, you can toggle between Cents per km and Logbook for each vehicle to see which method gives you the higher deduction. See [Vehicle deduction methods](faq/vehicle_deduction_methods) for details.
- **Review before lodging.** These reports are a record-keeping aid — always have your accountant or tax agent review the figures before you lodge with the ATO. The estimated tax calculation is an approximation and may not reflect your full tax situation.
- **Save your reports.** Generated reports can be shared via email, messaging apps, or saved to your device's files.

*For official guidance on BAS lodgement and individual tax returns, visit the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
