# ATO 5-Year Record Keeping Requirement

Under Australian tax law, businesses must keep accurate records of all transactions, invoices, receipts, and logbooks that relate to their tax affairs.

**You must keep these records for a minimum of 5 years.**

The 5-year period starts from the date you lodge your tax return for that financial year. 

FareKeep helps you comply with this requirement by securely storing your data and attachments. We strongly recommend enabling Cloud Backup to ensure your records are safe even if you lose your physical device.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
