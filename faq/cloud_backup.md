# How Cloud Backup Works

FareKeep securely backs up your database to your personal Google Drive. 
**We do not store your financial data on our servers.**

When you link your Google account and enable Cloud Backup:
- A secure folder is created in your Google Drive.
- FareKeep automatically syncs your data (transactions, logbooks, and settings) to this folder.
- If you lose your phone or switch devices, you can simply sign in with the same Google account on the new device and restore your data.

*Note: Receipts and photo attachments are also backed up to your Google Drive.*
