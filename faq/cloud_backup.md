# How Cloud Backup Works

FareKeep securely backs up your database to your personal Google Drive. 
**We do not store your financial data on our servers.**

When you link your Google account and use the Cloud Backup feature:
- A dedicated `FareKeepApp/Backups` folder is created in your Google Drive.
- You can manually back up your data (transactions, logbooks, tax form entries, settings, and receipt attachments) at any time by tapping the backup button.
- Each backup is compressed into a ZIP file for fast and reliable upload.
- If you lose your phone or switch devices, you can sign in with the same Google account on the new device and restore your data from the most recent backup.

## Restoring from Backup

1. Sign in with your Google account on the new device.
2. Go to **Settings → Backup & Restore**.
3. Select the backup file you want to restore.
4. Tap **Restore** to bring all your data back.

## Deleting Backups

You can delete individual backup files directly from the **Backup & Restore** screen. This removes the file from your Google Drive.
