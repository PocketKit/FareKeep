# Exporting Trips & Transactions

FareKeep lets you export your trip history and transaction records as PDF documents. This is useful for sharing records with your accountant, keeping offline copies, or printing them for your files.

## Exporting Logbook Trips

1. Go to the **Logbooks** tab and tap on a logbook to view its trips.
2. Tap the **download** icon in the top-right corner of the trips screen.
3. A dialog will appear asking you to select:
   - **Start Date** — the beginning of the period you want to export (defaults to one year ago).
   - **End Date** — the end of the period (defaults to today).
4. Tap **Generate PDF**.
5. The PDF will open directly on your device. From there, you can save it, print it, or share it via email or messaging apps.

The exported PDF includes a summary of all trips in the selected period, along with odometer readings, distances, and business/personal classifications.

## Exporting Transactions

1. Go to the **Transactions** tab.
2. Tap the **download** icon in the top-right corner.
3. A dialog will appear asking you to select:
   - **Start Date** and **End Date** — the period to export.
   - **Filter** — choose to export Income only, Expenses only, or All transactions.
4. Tap **Generate PDF**.
5. The PDF will open directly on your device.

The exported PDF includes a detailed listing of each transaction with amounts, GST, categories, and claimable percentages.

## Tips

- **Export before archiving.** If you plan to [archive a financial year](archives), it's a good idea to export your trips and transactions first so you have a standalone copy.
- **Share with your accountant.** The PDFs are formatted professionally and include all the information your tax agent typically needs.
