# Managing Employment Profiles

FareKeep lets you track your employment alongside your sole-trader or ride-share business, so everything is in one place when tax time comes.

## Adding an Employment Profile

Go to **Settings → Employment & Salary** and tap the **Add Employment** button. Fill in:

- **Employer Name** (required) — the name of your employer.
- **ABN** (optional) — your employer's Australian Business Number.
- **Job Title** — your role (e.g. "Software Developer", "Registered Nurse").
- **Hourly Rate** (optional) — used for reference only; it does not affect tax calculations.
- **Pay Cycle** — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly. This helps you track paychecks.
- **Tax-Free Threshold** — whether you claim the $18,200 tax-free threshold from this employer. You should claim it from only one employer.
- **WFH Eligible** — toggle this on if you regularly work from home in this role.
- **Start Date** — the date you began this job.

Once saved, you can log WFH hours, record employment expenses, track asset depreciation, and log paychecks against this profile.

## Active vs. Archived Jobs

- **Active** jobs appear in the "Active Jobs" tab and can have new WFH hours, expenses, and paychecks recorded against them.
- **Archived / Closed** jobs appear in the second tab. When you leave a job, you can close or archive it. All historical data (WFH logs, expenses, paychecks) is preserved for tax purposes and will still be included in your tax reports.

To archive a job, open the employment profile and change the status to "Closed". You can set an end date and the job will move to the Archived tab.

## Dual-Job Tax-Free Threshold Warning

If you have two or more active jobs and both are set to claim the tax-free threshold, FareKeep will display a prominent **ATO Dual-Job Tax Threshold Warning**. The ATO recommends claiming the tax-free threshold from only your primary employer to avoid under-withholding tax during the year.

## Free Tier vs. Premium Tier

- **Free Tier:** You can manage **1 employment profile** at no cost.
- **Premium Tier:** You can manage **unlimited employment profiles**, which is essential if you hold multiple concurrent jobs or frequently change employers.

When you attempt to add a second employment profile on the Free Tier, FareKeep will prompt you to upgrade to Premium.
