# How to Close a Logbook

When you no longer need to track trips for a particular vehicle — perhaps you've sold it, changed jobs, or stopped using it for business — you can close the logbook to stop it from appearing in your active records.

## How Closing Works

Closing a logbook is permanent. Once closed, the logbook is marked with a **Closed** badge and will no longer prompt you for new trips or odometer snapshots.

### To close an open-ended logbook:

1. Go to the **Logbooks** tab and tap on the logbook you want to close.
2. Tap the **Edit** (pencil) icon in the top-right corner.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap the **Close Logbook** button.
4. Enter the **closing date** (the last day you used the vehicle for business).
5. Enter the **final odometer reading** (your car's dashboard reading on that date).
6. Tap **Confirm** to close the logbook.

### 12-Week ATO logbooks

12-week logbooks close automatically when their 12-week period ends. You do not need to close them manually. Once the end date passes, the logbook is considered complete, and FareKeep will use its calculated business-use percentage for up to 5 years (as permitted by the ATO).

## What Happens After Closing?

- The logbook displays a **Closed** badge on the Logbooks screen.
- You will no longer be asked for odometer snapshots for this logbook.
- Your existing trips and data remain intact — nothing is deleted.
- The logbook's business-use percentage can still be used for expense claims on transactions dated within the period the logbook was active (see [Using a closed logbook for expenses](closing_logbook_and_expenses)).

## Example

You've been driving for Uber using an open-ended logbook since January 2024. In March 2026, you sell the car. You would close the logbook with a closing date of March 2026 and enter the odometer reading from the day you handed over the keys.

*For official information on the logbook method, visit the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
