# How Automatic Claimable % Works

When entering a vehicle expense (like Fuel or Repairs), you can choose to enter the claimable percentage manually, or let FareKeep calculate it automatically based on your logbook.

If you choose **Automatic**, here is how it works:
- First, [link the expense to a vehicle](vehicle_linked_expenses) using the **Related to vehicle?** dropdown in the transaction form.
- The app connects the expense to the selected logbook for that vehicle.
- When you generate a GST or Tax report at the end of the period, FareKeep looks at all the trips in that logbook.
- It calculates the final **Business Use Percentage** (Total Business KM / Total KM).
- This final percentage is automatically applied to the expense to determine your exact tax deduction.

This ensures your deductions are always perfectly accurate based on your actual driving data, without you having to do the math.

## What if I choose Manual instead?

If you toggle on **Enter Manually** and type in a percentage (e.g., 60%), FareKeep will use that exact figure. This is useful if you already know your approximate business-use split or if the expense isn't tied to a specific logbook.

Even with a manual percentage, you can still link the expense to a vehicle — it will be grouped under that vehicle's motor vehicle deductions in your Tax Summary Report.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
