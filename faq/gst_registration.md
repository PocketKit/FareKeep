# What is GST Registration?

When you register for Goods and Services Tax (GST), you collect 1/11th of your sales price for the ATO, and you can claim back the GST you pay on your business expenses.

## For Rideshare & Delivery Drivers
The ATO requires all rideshare/ride-sourcing drivers (e.g., Uber, Didi) to **register for GST before their first trip**. There is no minimum income threshold.

## For Other Businesses
For most other business types, you only need to register for GST when your gross business income (turnover) reaches **$75,000** or more per year.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
