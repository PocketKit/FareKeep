# Logbook Modes: 12-Week vs Open-Ended

FareKeep offers two ways to maintain a vehicle logbook for tax deduction purposes.

## 12-Week ATO Method
This is the standard ATO logbook method.
- You record **every single trip** (business and personal) for 12 continuous weeks.
- At the end, the app calculates your **Business Use Percentage**.
- The ATO allows you to use this established percentage for up to **5 years** (provided your usage doesn't change by more than 10%).
- After 12 weeks, you stop recording personal trips.

## Open-Ended Method
This method is useful if your business use fluctuates wildly or you prefer to track everything continuously.
- There is no 12-week limit.
- You continue recording trips forever.
- Your business use percentage is calculated dynamically based on the specific financial year's data.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
