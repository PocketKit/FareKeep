# Guest vs Signed-in Accounts

When you first install FareKeep, you start as a **Guest**. 

## Guest Account
- All your data is securely stored **locally on your device**.
- You have full access to all free features.
- If you lose your phone or delete the app, your data cannot be recovered.

## Signed-in Account
By signing in with Apple or Google, you unlock additional capabilities:
- **Cloud Backup:** You can securely link your Google Drive to automatically back up your data.
- **Cross-Device Sync:** Easily restore your data on a new device by signing into the same account.
- **Promo Codes:** You must be signed in to redeem promotional codes for Premium access.

*Note: Even when signed in, your financial data is never stored on FareKeep's servers. It remains securely on your device and your personal Google Drive.*
