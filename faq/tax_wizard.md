# Using the Tax Wizard

The Tax Wizard is a guided, step-by-step form that helps you prepare your annual Tax Summary Report. It collects additional information that FareKeep needs to generate a comprehensive summary of your tax position for the financial year.

## How to Access the Tax Wizard

1. Go to **Settings → Tax Summary Report**.
2. Select the **Financial Year** you want to prepare.
3. Tap the **Open Tax Form** button.

## Steps in the Tax Wizard

The Tax Wizard guides you through four steps:

### Step 1: Triage

Tell FareKeep about your situation so the form is tailored to you. Toggle on the items that apply:

- **Do you earn a salary or wages as an employee?** — If you also work as an employee alongside your business.
- **Did you earn any bank interest?** — Interest from savings accounts or term deposits.
- **Do you have any tools or equipment to depreciate?** — Work-related assets that lose value over time.
- **Do you have a HELP/HECS student loan?** — If you have an outstanding student loan, FareKeep will estimate your HELP repayment.
- **Did you pay any PAYG Instalments directly to the ATO?** — Quarterly tax payments made during the year.

### Step 2: Vehicles

For each vehicle used in your business, FareKeep will show you the deduction amount under two ATO-approved methods:

- **Cents per Kilometre** — a fixed rate per business kilometre (capped at 5,000 km per vehicle per year).
- **Logbook** — based on your actual vehicle running costs multiplied by your logbook business-use percentage.

FareKeep pre-selects the method that gives you the **higher deduction** by default. You can switch between methods to compare. See [Vehicle deduction methods](vehicle_deduction_methods) for more detail.

### Step 3: Details

Enter the dollar amounts for any items you toggled on in Step 1:

- **Salary or wages** and **Tax withheld** from your PAYG summary.
- **Bank interest** earned during the financial year.
- **Depreciation** amount for work-related assets.
- **PAYG Instalments** paid directly to the ATO.

### Step 4: Review & ATO Audit

This step displays a comprehensive summary of your tax position, including:

- **Employment Income:** Aggregated salary, tax withheld, allowances, and lump sum payments across all your active and archived employment profiles for the financial year.
- **Itemised Work Deductions (D1–D10):** A breakdown of your total employment-related deductions, including D1 Car expenses, D2 Travel, D3 Uniform/Laundry, D4 Self-Education, D5 WFH (total hours × 67c/hr) and asset depreciation, D9 Donations, and D10 Tax Affairs.
- **ATO Compliance Audit:** FareKeep automatically runs a series of compliance checks against known ATO rules and flags any issues:
  - **5,000 km Cap:** Warns if your total cents-per-km car claim exceeds the statutory 5,000 km limit.
  - **$300 Receipt Limit:** Warns if D3 or D5 expenses exceed $300 without documented receipts.
  - **Duplicate WFH Check:** Warns if more than 16 hours of WFH are logged on a single day across multiple jobs.

A green "100% ATO Compliant" badge appears if no issues are found. Otherwise, specific warnings are listed with ATO references so you can review and correct them.

## Saving Your Progress

You can tap **Save Draft** at any time to save your progress and return later. Your entries are saved locally on your device.

## What Happens Next?

After completing the Tax Wizard, return to the Tax Report screen. FareKeep will combine your wizard entries, employment profile data, WFH logs, employment expenses, and asset depreciation with your recorded business transactions and logbook data to generate a complete **Tax Summary PDF** — including ATO label mapping, an itemised D1–D10 deduction breakdown, an ATO compliance audit report, and an estimated tax outcome.

> **Important:** The estimated tax calculation is an approximation only. Always have your accountant or tax agent review the figures before lodging with the ATO.

*For official guidance on lodging your tax return, visit the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
