# What does Claimable % mean?

When you incur an expense that is used for both business and personal reasons, you can only claim the **business portion** as a tax deduction. This is your "Claimable Percentage."

## Example
If your mobile phone bill is $100, and you use your phone 60% of the time for business calls/apps and 40% for personal use:
- Your Claimable % is **60%**.
- You can claim **$60** as a business expense.

If an expense is purely for business (e.g., rideshare commission fees), the Claimable % is **100%**.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
