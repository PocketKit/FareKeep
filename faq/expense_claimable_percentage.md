# What does Claimable % mean?

When you incur an expense that is used for both business and personal reasons, you can only claim the **business portion** as a tax deduction. This is your "Claimable Percentage."

## Example
If your mobile phone bill is $100, and you use your phone 60% of the time for business calls/apps and 40% for personal use:
- Your Claimable % is **60%**.
- You can claim **$60** as a business expense.

If an expense is purely for business (e.g., rideshare commission fees), the Claimable % is **100%**.

## Options in FareKeep

When adding an expense, you have three claimable options:

1. **100% Claimable for Business** — toggle this on if the expense is entirely for business use.
2. **Automatic** — FareKeep calculates the claimable percentage from your logbook's business-use data. See [How automatic claimable % works](automatic_claimable_calculation).
3. **Enter Manually** — type in a specific percentage if you know the business-use split.

## Vehicle Expenses

If the expense is related to a vehicle, make sure to also select the relevant vehicle using the **Related to vehicle?** dropdown. This ensures the expense is correctly grouped under motor vehicle deductions (ATO Item D1) rather than general expenses (Item D5) when you generate your Tax Summary Report. See [Linking expenses to a vehicle](vehicle_linked_expenses) for details.

*For official information, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
