# How to Manage Categories & Payment Methods

FareKeep allows you to customise the options available in dropdown menus across the app (like Expense Categories and Payment Methods).

Navigate to **Settings → Master Data** to view all available lists. From there, you can:
- **Add:** Create new categories tailored to your specific business needs.
- **Delete:** Remove categories you no longer use (note: default system categories cannot be deleted).

Custom entries you add are specific to your active **Business Profile**. If you switch profiles, you won't see the custom entries from your other business.
