# What are Odometer Snapshots?

Odometer snapshots are periodic recordings of your car's total dashboard odometer reading. 

The ATO requires you to record your odometer at the start and end of the logbook period (and the start and end of every financial year) to verify the total kilometers your car has traveled.

By providing periodic snapshots, FareKeep can accurately calculate your exact business use percentage using the **Exact Method**, rather than relying solely on the sum of your individually entered trips, which can sometimes be inaccurate.
