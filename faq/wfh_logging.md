# Logging Work From Home (WFH) Hours

If you work from home as part of your employment, FareKeep helps you track your WFH hours so you can claim the ATO's **fixed rate of 67 cents per hour** at tax time.

## How the ATO 67c/hr Fixed Rate Works

From 1 July 2022, the ATO revised fixed rate method allows you to claim **67 cents for each hour you work from home**. This rate covers:

- Electricity and gas
- Internet and mobile phone (work-related portion)
- Stationery and computer consumables

**Important:** The 67c/hr rate does **not** cover depreciation of home office equipment (e.g. laptops, desks, monitors). Those items should be tracked separately under [Employment Expenses & Asset Depreciation](employment_expenses_and_assets).

## Accessing the WFH Calendar

1. Go to **Settings → Employment & Salary**.
2. Tap on your active employment profile.
3. Tap **WFH Calendar**.

You will see a monthly calendar view. Days with logged WFH hours are highlighted.

## Quick Mode (8 Hours)

Tap any date on the calendar to instantly log a standard **8-hour WFH day**. This is the fastest way to record your WFH hours if you work a regular schedule.

## Detailed Mode

For more precise tracking, tap the **pencil icon** on a date to enter:

- **Start Time** — when you started working from home.
- **End Time** — when you finished.
- **Break Duration** — total break time (e.g. lunch). This is subtracted from your total hours.

FareKeep calculates the net work hours automatically (End – Start – Break).

## Monthly & Year-to-Date Summary

At the top of the WFH Calendar, you can see:

- **Total WFH hours this month**
- **Total WFH deduction this month** (hours × 67c)
- **Year-to-date WFH hours and deductions**

## How WFH Hours Flow Into Your Tax Return

When you use the Tax Wizard, your total WFH hours across all employment profiles are aggregated and mapped to ATO label **D5 — Other work-related expenses**. The total deduction is calculated as:

> **Total WFH Hours × 67c = D5 WFH Deduction**

This is automatically included in the Itemised D1–D10 breakdown in your generated Tax Report PDF.

## Remote Config Rate

The 67c/hr rate is fetched from Firebase Remote Config, so if the ATO updates the rate in a future financial year, FareKeep can reflect the change without requiring an app update.
