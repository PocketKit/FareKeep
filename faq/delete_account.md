# How to Delete Your Account

If you no longer wish to use FareKeep or want to start fresh, you can permanently delete your signed-in account directly from the app. We provide two different deletion options depending on whether you want to keep your local data on your device or wipe everything completely.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting your cloud account is permanent. Any data stored in the cloud will be lost and cannot be recovered. Ensure you have backed up any necessary reports before proceeding.

### Steps to Delete Your Account

1. Open the FareKeep app.
2. Navigate to **Settings** from the bottom navigation bar.
3. Tap on **Account** to view your profile and account settings.
4. Scroll down to the bottom and tap to expand the **Danger Zone** section.
5. Choose one of the following deletion options:

#### Option A: Delete Cloud Account
- **What it does:** This option deletes your account from our secure cloud servers (your login credentials).
- **What it keeps:** All your local trips, transactions, and logbooks will **remain on your device**. Your exsisting backups will stay in your google drive. You can continue using the app as a Guest user.

#### Option B: Delete Account & Data
- **What it does:** This is a complete wipe. It deletes your cloud account **and** erases all local data stored on your device. Your exsisting backups from google drive will remain untouched (You can delete existing backups from google drive manually).
- **When to use this:** Choose this if you are completely uninstalling the app and want to ensure no trace of your data is left behind on the device or our servers.

> [!NOTE]
> If you are currently subscribed to a premium plan via the App Store or Google Play, deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. You must manage and cancel your active subscriptions directly through your device's subscription settings (Apple ID or Google Play Account).
