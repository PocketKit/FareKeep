# Saving Draft Transactions & Trips

Sometimes you might not have all the details you need to complete a transaction or log a trip right away. FareKeep allows you to save these records as **Drafts** so you don't lose any information.

## How to save a Draft

When creating a new **Transaction** (Income or Expense) or a **Trip**, you can click the **Save as Draft** button. This bypasses the strict validation rules, allowing you to save the record even if it is incomplete.

For example:
- You can snap a photo of a receipt and save the transaction as a draft without entering the amount or category.
- You can log the start odometer reading of a trip before you start driving, save it as a draft, and fill in the end odometer when you arrive.

## Identifying Drafts

Drafts are marked with a prominent yellow **DRAFT** badge in your lists. They will also display placeholders (like `--` for amounts or `-- km` for distances) for any information that is missing.

## Drafts in Reports

**Important:** Draft transactions and trips are considered incomplete and are **excluded** from all calculations and reports. 
- They do not count towards your total income or expenses.
- Draft trips do not affect your logbook business-use percentage.
- They are not included in your GST or Tax reports.

If you generate a GST or Tax report for a period that contains draft records, FareKeep will display a warning banner reminding you that incomplete items exist and have been excluded from the calculations. You must finalize these drafts to have them included in your final ATO reporting.
