# Employment Expenses & Asset Depreciation

FareKeep lets you record work-related expenses and track the depreciation of assets purchased for your employment. These are separate from your sole-trader or ride-share business expenses.

## Recording Employment Expenses

From your employment profile, tap **Expenses** to view and add work-related expenses. Each expense is categorised under ATO deduction labels:

| ATO Label | Category | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Work-related car expenses | Fuel, tolls, parking for work travel |
| **D2** | Work-related travel expenses | Flights, accommodation, meals during work trips |
| **D3** | Work-related clothing/laundry | Uniforms, protective clothing, laundry costs |
| **D4** | Work-related self-education | Course fees, textbooks, professional development |
| **D5** | Other work-related expenses | Union fees, tools, phone/internet (work portion) |

When adding a D1 (car) expense, you can optionally link it to an **Employment Vehicle** from your vehicle list.

## Employment Vehicles vs. Business Vehicles

FareKeep keeps employment vehicles completely separate from your business (sole-trader/ride-share) vehicles:

- **Business Vehicles** are managed from the main dashboard and are linked to logbooks and business trips.
- **Employment Vehicles** are managed from **Settings → Employment & Salary → Vehicles** and are used specifically for work-related car expense tracking.

You can archive (soft-delete) an employment vehicle when you sell it or stop using it for work. Archived vehicles cannot be selected for new expenses, but their historical records are preserved.

## Asset Depreciation

If you purchase items for your employment (e.g. a laptop, desk, or monitor), you can track their depreciation over time.

From your employment profile, tap **Assets** to view and add items.

### ATO Depreciation Methods

FareKeep supports four ATO-approved depreciation methods:

| Method | When it applies | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Immediate Deduction** | Cost ≤ $300 | 100% deductible in the year of purchase |
| **Low-Value Pool** | Cost < $1,000 | 18.75% in Year 1, then 37.5% each subsequent year |
| **Prime Cost (Straight Line)** | Cost > $300 | Even annual deductions over the asset's effective life |
| **Diminishing Value** | Cost > $300 | Accelerated deductions (higher in early years, lower later) |

### What You Enter

When adding an asset:

- **Item Name** — e.g. "Dell Laptop", "Standing Desk"
- **Purchase Date** — when you bought the item
- **Purchase Cost** — the total price paid
- **Effective Life (years)** — the ATO-determined useful life (e.g. 4 years for a laptop)
- **Employment Use %** — the percentage you use it for work (e.g. 80%)
- **Depreciation Method** — choose from the methods above

### How Depreciation Flows Into Your Tax Return

FareKeep's **Asset Depreciation Calculator** automatically calculates the claimable amount for each financial year, prorated by the number of days you owned the asset in that year and multiplied by your employment use percentage.

The total depreciation across all assets is mapped to ATO label **D5 — Other work-related expenses** and appears in the Itemised D1–D10 breakdown in your Tax Report PDF.
