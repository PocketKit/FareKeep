# Attaching Receipts & Documents

Good record-keeping means holding onto your receipts. FareKeep makes this easy by letting you attach photos and documents directly to your transactions — no more shoeboxes full of fading paper receipts.

## How to Attach a Receipt

When creating or editing a transaction, you'll see an **Attachments** section at the bottom of the form. Tap the attachment button to choose from:

- **Camera** — take a photo of a physical receipt using your phone's camera.
- **Document Scanner** — use your device's built-in document scanner to automatically detect the edges of a receipt, crop it, and save a clean image.
- **Photo Library** — select an existing image from your phone's photo gallery.
- **PDF File** — pick a PDF document from your device's file storage (useful for digital invoices or emailed receipts).

You can attach **multiple files** to a single transaction — for example, a receipt photo and a related invoice PDF.

## Storage & Compression

To save space on your device, FareKeep automatically:
- Compresses photo images to **WebP format** (a modern, smaller image format).
- Stores attachments in the app's private directory on your device.

If you find that older attachments are taking up too much space, you can use the **App Optimizer** (Settings → App Optimizer) to batch-convert older images to WebP format and audit large PDF files.

## Backup

When you use [Cloud Backup](cloud_backup), your receipt attachments are included in the backup. They are uploaded alongside your database to your personal Google Drive, so you won't lose them if you switch devices.

## ATO Requirements

The ATO requires you to keep records (including receipts) for **5 years** from the date you lodge your tax return. FareKeep helps you meet this requirement by securely storing your receipts digitally. See [ATO 5-Year Record Keeping](ato_record_keeping) for more details.

*Tip: Photograph receipts as soon as you receive them — thermal paper receipts fade over time and can become unreadable.*
