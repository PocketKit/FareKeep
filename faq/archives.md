# Archiving Financial Years

Once a financial year has ended and you have lodged your tax return, you can **Archive** the year in FareKeep.

Archiving a financial year:
- **Locks** all transactions and logbooks for that period, preventing accidental edits or deletions.
- Removes old receipt attachments from your device to save storage space (if you choose).
- Generates a final, permanent export of your data for that year.

You can view archived data at any time, but you cannot change it without un-archiving it first.

*For official information on record keeping, always consult the [ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au).*
